# Game Mechanics / Core Ideas

## Details

The game would be split into 3 core parts

1. Land Ownership

2. Auction and Land Purchases / Selling

3. Gambling

## Land Ownership

### Building

Whenever you get a property, you can either

1. Build a house
2. Build an apartment complex (expensive)
3. Or keep it bare

If you choose to build a house or apartment, you must install utilities. People could also ask you to install some things for other types of land.

Bare land generates the least amount of income, and it can change the most.
Houses generates the middle most amount of income, and it can change a little bit.
Apartment complexes generate the most amount of income, and it rarely changes on its own.

### Upgrading

Depending on the type of building on the land, you can upgrade 3 basic things:

1. Looks (only applicable to Houses and Apartments)
  - House keeping
  - Making it more good looking
2. Advertising
  - Get more people to learn about your business and ask for places to live
3. Money generation
  - Includes amount to pay (rent upgrades)

### Renting

You can rent your land to people. You can also evict people. But if you evict to many people, they may not want to try to live on your land.
You have to keep a good reputation, like fixing broken things, upgrading looks every once in a while, etc.
You gain money from people who are renting every in-game day or week.
Includes setting up when to pay amounts, i.e. if cost = $100 / m, you can ask for $25 every week


## Auction and Land Purchases / Selling

### Buying Land

Buying land works by opening a menu and seeing if any places are for sale.
Places refresh every 5-10 in-game days.

### Selling Land

You can sell land for 90% of the starting cost + 95% of the upgrades.

### Auctions

Auctions happen randomly, but one auction happens every 10 in-game days (meaning you could only get one auction in 10 days, or you could get 100 auctions in 10 days, but the more, the rarer).
