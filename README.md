# Landlords-Luck
A pixel art game about renting land and houses to people!

https://gist.github.com/FaizeenHoque/0709576cc598721df07fb62d2430e6fe

A pixl art 2d game about gambling!!!
You start with different properties, and you spend money renting and upgrading them. 
You can rent them to different people, kick people out if they aren't paying you or are trashing the place. Then you could go over to the neighborhood casino and gamble your money to get more money or land!
